Try editing the sample table below. For an introduction, see [Annotation Table](/nfdi4plants.knowledgebase/swate/annotation-table/).

- Double-click a cell to edit it. Press **Enter** to keep your change or **Escape** to cancel.
- Right-click a cell to explore its context menu, including **Details**.
- Select **Reset sample data** to restore the original three sample rows.

Changes are temporary and disappear when you reset or reload the playground.
