<Steps>

1. Open the ARC in ARCitISA
2. In the left sidebar, open the `Validation packages` menu
3. Check the box next to `invenio` and click "Submit"
4. Save and upload your changes via the `Git` menu
5. Open your ARC in the DataHUB to inspect the validation results.

</Steps>
