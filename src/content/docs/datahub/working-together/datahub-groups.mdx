In the DataHUB, groups provide a shared space and access management. 
This is useful for a lab, institute, or consortium. Members' access is based on their group role.

Groups can contain subgroups for teams or projects with separate membership. Access may be inherited by projects in the group hierarchy.

:::tip[Groups and Projects]
Users sometimes confuse the DataHUB terminology for "groups" and "projects". 
Here, ARCs are called **projects**, while a **group** is a group of users.
:::


## Set up a lab group

- Keep at least two **Owners** (for example, the PI and a staff scientist or data manager).
- Assign a **Maintainer** to handle routine membership and project administration.
- Give other members the lowest role they need: **Developer** to contribute, **Reporter** to read.

Example roles for a lab group:

| Person or role | Suggested group role | Typical responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PI | Owner | Oversees the group and appoints administrators |
| Staff scientist or data manager | Owner or Maintainer | Administers the group |
| Lab member | Developer | Contributes to ARCs |
| Reviewer or consultant | Reporter | Reads ARC contents |

:::tip
If someone only needs access to one ARC, invite them to that ARC instead of giving access to the whole group.
:::

## Roles for group members

- **Guest** – Cannot read ARC contents.
- **Reporter** – Can read ARC contents.
- **Developer** – Can read and contribute to ARCs; may create projects in the group if group settings allow it.
- **Maintainer** – Can contribute and manage group membership.
- **Owner** – Can manage group settings and membership.

:::tip[Permissions]
For exact permissions, see [DataHUB roles](/nfdi4plants.knowledgebase/datahub/working-together/datahub-roles).
:::

## Find or create a group

Open the [groups panel](https://git.nfdi4plants.org/explore/groups/) to view your groups or explore visible groups.

![](@images/datahub/datahub-group-panel.drawio.png)

<Steps>

1. Select **Groups** to view your groups or explore available groups.
2. Select **New group** to create one.

</Steps>

![](@images/datahub/datahub-group-panel-create.drawio.png)

<Steps>

1. Enter a group name and check its URL (namespace).
2. Choose visibility.
3. Add initial members and assign roles.
4. Create the group.

</Steps>

To manage members later, open the group and select **Manage** → **Members**. Invite members and assign roles, or remove access when it is no longer needed. Owners can manage group settings and membership.

## Choose the group's visibility

Groups can be:

- **Private** – Only explicitly authorized users can access the group.
- **Internal** – Signed-in DataHUB users can access it.
- **Public** – Anyone can access it without signing in.

Keep unpublished, sensitive, or restricted work private. Check institutional and project data-sharing requirements before making a group public.

<Steps>

1. Open the group.
2. Go to **Settings** → **General**.
3. Change the visibility setting and save.

</Steps>

Projects have separate visibility settings. Check both group and project visibility before sharing a link.

## Choose how to share an ARC

| What you need | Recommended approach | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start an ARC for ongoing lab work | Create or sync it in the group namespace | The group is the ARC's namespace and shared home. |
| Keep an existing ARC under team ownership | Transfer it to the group | Moves it from a personal to the group namespace. Useful when the lab needs to retain it. |
| Give a group access without changing the ARC's namespace | Invite the group to the ARC | The ARC remains in its current namespace; assign the appropriate role. |
| Develop a separate copy of an ARC | Fork it into the group | Creates a linked copy that can change independently. |

Developers can create projects (ARCs) in a group unless the group restricts project creation. An Owner can check **Settings** → **General** → **Permissions and group features** → **Allowed to create projects**. Creating a subgroup requires Maintainer or Owner access. In [ARCitect](/nfdi4plants.knowledgebase/arcitect), choose the group namespace when syncing.

- **Student leaves:** Transfer any ARC the lab needs to retain to the lab group.
- **Consortium project:** Invite a partner group to an ARC that stays under the partner's ownership; create or transfer an ARC to the consortium group for a shared canonical copy.

See [Inviting collaborators](/nfdi4plants.knowledgebase/datahub/working-together/datahub-invite-collaborators) and [Fork an ARC](/nfdi4plants.knowledgebase/datahub/arc-features/datahub-arc-fork) for details.
